Status: in-code

## In words

Locale is the language of the screens: Arabic and English. Arabic layout is right to left. This is separate from the retrieval golden set, which still includes Chinese, Hindi, and French. Changing the screen language does not re-embed any file.

## Owns

Locale owns Arabic and English chrome and right-to-left layout. Types: `LocaleService`, `UiLocale`. Store key `recall.locale`.

## Does not own

It does not own the golden retrieval languages.
