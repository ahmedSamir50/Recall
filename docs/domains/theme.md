Status: in-code

## In words

Theme is System, Dark, or Light. System follows the operating-system appearance. The choice is stored on the device and applied by the shell snapshot. It has no effect on search.

## Owns

Theme owns System, Dark, and Light. Types: `ThemeService`, `ThemePreference`.
